# Privacy Policy — Mine Puzzle

**Last updated:** 5 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Zbigniew Łobocki** (“we”, “us”) handles information in the mobile game **Mine Puzzle** (the “App”).

Mine Puzzle is designed to work **offline**. We do **not** run ads, analytics SDKs, or account systems in the App, and we do **not** sell personal data. The App is free to download. We may offer optional paid add-ons through Google Play in the future; that would not by itself change how gameplay data is stored (still on your device unless we update this Policy).

## 2. What data the App stores

The App stores data **only on your device**, using local storage (for example Android SharedPreferences). This may include:

- Progress of an active game (board, pieces, score, round, items)
- Local top-10 high scores and the **display name** you type for a score
- Settings such as music/SFX volume and soundtrack preference
- Whether you have accepted the Terms of Use

We do **not** require you to create an account, and we do **not** knowingly collect names, emails, or payment details through the App for our own servers.

If you buy an optional add-on later, **Google Play** processes the payment under Google’s terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your full payment card details.

## 3. What we do **not** collect

As of this version, the App does **not**:

- Send gameplay or score data to our servers
- Use advertising identifiers for ads
- Use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
- Access your contacts, photos, microphone, or location

If a future update adds online features, ads, or analytics, we will update this Policy and (where required) ask for fresh consent.

## 4. Why we process this data (GDPR legal bases)

If you are in the EEA/UK, local on-device storage for gameplay and settings is based on:

- **Contract / requested service** — to provide the game you chose to play (progress, scores, settings)
- **Legitimate interests** — to remember that you accepted the Terms, so we do not show the acceptance screen every launch

High-score **names** are optional text you enter; they stay on your device.

## 5. Sharing

We do not share App data with third parties because we do not receive it on our servers.

Google Play may process information about downloads, installs, and (if applicable) purchases under Google’s own policies when you use the Play Store. That processing is controlled by Google, not by us through the App.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you clear the App’s storage, uninstall the App, or use an in-app action that resets progress (for example starting a new game that replaces a save).

Uninstalling the App deletes local App data under normal Android behaviour.

## 7. Children

The App is a casual puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. It does not include social features or advertising. If you are a parent/guardian and believe a child has provided personal data to us outside the App (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Your rights (EEA/UK and similar laws)

Because we do not hold identifiable App data on our servers, most rights are exercised on-device (clear storage / uninstall).

You may still contact us at **Exquisitor@wp.pl** to ask questions about this Policy, request clarification, or ask us to delete any personal data you sent us by email.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Poland: Urząd Ochrony Danych Osobowych — UODO).

## 9. International transfers

The App does not transfer your gameplay data to our servers. Any contact you initiate by email will be received in the systems we use for that inbox.

## 10. Changes

We may update this Policy when the App or our practices change. The “Last updated” date will change, and material changes will be reflected in the Play Store listing / in-App notice where appropriate.
